Rulio Get the App

Ruler, Floor Plan & Level

Rulio is an iPhone app that turns the camera into a tape measure. Point, tap two points and read the size in metres or feet. It also draws floor plans with area and perimeter, and measures height, volume and angle. No account is needed and camera frames never leave the phone.

What Rulio does

  • Tape — point-to-point distance with a live readingPoint-to-point distance in two taps. Aim the circle at the start, tap +, move to the end, tap + and read the length live in metres or feet and inches. Undo steps back one point.
  • Floor plan — tap the room's corners, get wall lengths, area and perimeter, share as PDFTap the corners of a room to get wall lengths, floor area and perimeter. Share the plan as a PDF.
  • Area — floors, walls, rugs, anything flatOutline a floor, wall or rug and tap Close outline to get its area.
  • Volume — outline a box or a sofa, tap its top, get the volumeOutline the base of a box, tap the top and get the volume.
  • Height — from the floor to the top of a door, a shelf or a ceilingMeasure from the floor to the top of a door, shelf or ceiling.
  • Angle — corners, slopes and cutsCorners, slopes and cuts read in degrees.
  • Ruler and LevelAn on-screen ruler in cm and inches, calibrated with any bank card, and a bubble level for surfaces and edges.
  • Projects and photosEach saved measurement keeps a photo with the lines drawn in. Group measurements into projects such as kitchen or moving day, and share a size, a photo or a whole project.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is Rulio?

Camera measurements are estimates. Check critical sizes, such as cutting material, with a physical tape. On iPhones with LiDAR Rulio uses the scene mesh to place points on real surfaces.

Does Rulio need an internet connection?

Measuring happens on the phone and camera frames never leave it. There is no account or sign-in.

Is Rulio free?

No. All tools are in Rulio Pro, with a 3-day free trial on the weekly and yearly plans. Apple's built-in Measure app is free if you only need a quick length.

Can I measure a whole room?

Yes. The Floor plan tool lets you tap the room's corners and gives wall lengths, area and perimeter, which you can share as a PDF.

Can I use feet and inches?

Yes. Pick metric or imperial at the start and switch the ruler between cm and inches.

Can I save and group measurements?

Yes. Every saved measurement keeps a photo with the lines drawn in, and you can group them into projects.

How do I cancel?

Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions › Rulio. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.
