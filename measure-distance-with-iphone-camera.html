How to Measure a Distance With Your iPhone Camera

Updated October 2026 · By the Rulio developer

Short answer. Open an AR measuring app, aim at the start point, tap, then move to the end point and tap again. Rulio does this on iPhone: the Tape tool shows a live reading in metres or feet and inches, and the measurement can be saved with a photo.

Step by step

  1. Open the Tape toolPick Tape in Rulio and point the camera at the surface. Follow the on-screen coaching until it finds the surface.
  2. Mark the startStep 1 of 2: aim the circle at the start and tap +.
  3. Mark the endStep 2 of 2: move to the end and tap +. The length appears live.
  4. Save itSave the measurement; it keeps a photo with the line drawn in. Use Undo to redo a point.

Frequently asked questions

Is it as exact as a tape measure?

No. Camera measurements are estimates, so check critical sizes with a physical tape.

Can I switch to inches?

Yes. Choose metric or imperial in the app.
