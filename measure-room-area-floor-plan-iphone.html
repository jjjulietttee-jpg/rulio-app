How to Measure a Room's Area and Make a Floor Plan on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Rulio developer

Short answer. Walk to each corner of the room and tap it, then close the outline. Rulio shows each wall length, the floor area and the perimeter, and shares the plan as a PDF.

Step by step

  1. Open Floor planPick Floor plan in Rulio and aim at the first corner of the floor.
  2. Tap each cornerTap + at every corner; Undo removes the last point.
  3. Close the outlineTap Close outline when you are back at the start.
  4. Share the PDFRead wall lengths, area and perimeter, then share the plan as a PDF or save it to a project.
